- **A sink-based ratchet for outbound HTTP calls across `src/soup_cli` (#1547 by @Areen-09 in #1646).**
  An AST ratchet verifies every outbound HTTP call (`httpx` methods and clients,
  `urllib.request` openers and callers, and `requests` methods) against a declared
  ledger table of known gates and justifications, replacing shape-based checks with
  fail-closed bidirectional enforcement. `validate_electricity_map_endpoint` now routes
  through `net_guard.LOOPBACK_HOSTS` and `refuse_private_ip_literal`, closing SSRF
  bypasses on abbreviated IPv4 and RFC 6598 carrier-grade NAT.
